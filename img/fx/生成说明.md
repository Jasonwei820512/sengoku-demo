# 任务 72 规则美术素材

使用内置 ImageGen。栅栏、雾、马头、铁链、火苗、旗、瞄准圈、炮弹和卷轴来自一张透明图集，落地烟尘单独生成。用 Core Graphics 裁切和缩放，保留透明通道。章节缩略图复用现有七张开场图。

## 图集提示词

> Transparent game sprite atlas for Japanese Sengoku mobile puzzle game. Hand-painted impasto, warm directional light, deep brown outlines, aged gold and vermilion accents. Square canvas in a strict evenly spaced 3 × 3 grid with nine isolated motifs centered within their cells and generous empty gutters so each third can be cropped independently. Row 1: front view pointed wooden palisade tied with two straight horizontal straw ropes; soft white wispy fog cloud; ochre horse head facing left with dusty arrow pointing left. Row 2: two thin diagonal iron chains forming an X and a small iron padlock at crossing, lots of transparent space; a slim horizontal strip of small orange flames; unfurled blank cloth military flag with golden edging. Row 3: rough vermilion brush targeting ring with four charcoal scorch marks; a black iron sphere at bottom right trailing orange fire towards upper left; rolled ivory scroll with red cord and bronze padlock. No text, no ground planes or background. Objects readable at small size.

## 落地烟尘提示词

> One small Japanese Sengoku puzzle-game impact effect sprite, isolated on clear transparent background, wide horizontal crescent-shaped puff of warm ashy smoke emerging along the ground, a handful of tiny orange sparks spraying upward. Hand-painted thick brushwork, warm amber light and deep umber shadows. No objects, no text, no floor, no blood. Readable at 60px. Keep complete motif comfortably centered.
